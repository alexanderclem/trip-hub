# Surface brief: the public website (home first; how-it-works, pricing, operators, help, legal share it)

Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: the organized friend deciding to start a group trip, arriving on an iPhone; friends who already hold an invite get a visible door. Action: Create a trip; secondary Join trip (/app#join). Proof: the product's own loop, shown on real places from the founder's spring-break 2027 Guatemala trip (Antigua, Panajachel, San Marcos, lancha routes from seed/guatemala-2027); traveler names and plan details are labelled examples. Operators: one quiet band on home; fees and dashboards live on /operators and /pricing. No testimonials, counts or claims that don't exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the group's bag tag. The trip prints itself down one long tag: route codes, travelers, the group's choices, the plan, a stub you keep offline. It refuses the planner-category arrangement of a headline beside a phone screenshot over a grid of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Thermal tag stock (cool near-white) carrying ocean-ink print; huge condensed route codes (ANT → PAN); perforated tear lines and stub edges as the only dividers; barcodes; coral PRIORITY labels as the single loud colour, given only to the group's choice and the primary action; signal yellow for "still deciding". Confirmed prints solid, tentative prints half-toned, empty is a ghost tag. Dark mode is the same tag printed in reverse on ocean ink.

STORY: The visitor sees a real trip take shape: save places, the group votes and the favourite gets priority, it lands on the day-by-day routing, it keeps working with no signal, tickets and costs ride along as stubs, friends join by picking their name. They believe it's simpler than the group chat and that it's free for the group, and they create a trip or open their invite.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A routing strip down the left edge (GUA · ANT · PAN · SMR, the current stop lit). The main area is one tag face: tiny print header (STOWAWAY · GROUP TRIP · SPRING BREAK 2027), route codes ANT → PAN at display scale, then the h1 "Your group trip. All in one place." and the one-line explanation. Travelers print in one name at a time. Two perforated tear-off stubs: "Create a trip" (coral, primary) and "Got an invite link? Join trip". A barcode closes the tag. Phone: the strip becomes a top route bar, the codes stay huge, both stubs above the fold.

FORM: Luggage Tag, the assigned direction, candidate 3 of 7 on my resonance list. Raises: ghost-tag empty states (seven-segment), presence printed as events (BBS nightboard), one routing axis pins every section (deep dive), state as print density (signboard). Signature interaction: the vote; the visitor's vote prints with the group's, the winner is stamped PRIORITY and drops onto the routing strip. Motion grammar: print-in (line by line, a thermal head) and stamp; nothing loops; reduced motion shows the printed state. seed:7e95df9a1137a7d1
